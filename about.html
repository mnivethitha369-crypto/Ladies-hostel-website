About Us

Welcome to Our PSY QUEENS INN Ladies Hostel


At our PSY Ladies Hostel, we believe that a hostel should be more than just a place to stay—it should feel like a second home. We provide a safe, comfortable, and supportive environment for students, working professionals, and women seeking quality accommodation.


About Our Hostel

Established in 2018, our Ladies Hostel provides a safe, comfortable, and homely environment for students and working women. We focus on security, cleanliness, healthy food, and a peaceful atmosphere.

Located in the heart of the city, our hostel offers easy access to colleges, workplaces, hospitals, shopping centers, and public transportation.

Our Mission

To provide affordable and secure accommodation where every resident feels at home.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted PSY ladies hostel by maintaining high standards of safety and comfort.
